How to play

Objective

Each team has an energy core inside its HQ bunker. Push across the river, breach the bunker and destroy the enemy core while holding off attacks on yours. Explosives hit cores much harder than bullets.

Keyboard & mouse

Click the game to capture the mouse. WASD move · Mouse look · LMB fire · RMB aim down sights · Shift sprint · Space jump · R reload · 1/2/Q/wheel swap · G equipment · Tab scores · Esc pause

Touch

Left thumb moves; push all the way up to sprint. Drag anywhere on the right to look. Hold FIRE to shoot and AIM for the sights. Aim assist slows and nudges your view onto enemies near the crosshair.

Destruction

Nearly everything breaks. Cut a trunk and the tree falls (on whoever is under it). Knock out the base of a wall and the roof comes down. Red fuel barrels chain-react. Craters near the river flood.

Scoring

Eliminations 100, headshots +50, assists 40, multi-kills and streaks pay bonuses, ending an enemy streak pays 50, and every point of core damage is worth half a point. The match MVP is whoever scores most.

Tips

Canopies hide you from above, and bushes block sight lines. Suppressors keep you off the enemy minimap. Rockets and demo charges open new paths into bunkers. Refill ammo at your base's supply depot.
